Invest uses a narrow adapter that adds RSI(14), MACD, Bollinger Bands and ATR(14) to already-normalized OHLC research frames.

The adapter is feature-engineering only: it does not generate buy/sell orders, position sizes or personalized investment advice. The dependency stays optional.
